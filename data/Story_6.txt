There are many other types of Enabler stories, including: Refactoring and Spikes (as traditionally defined in XP) Building or improving development/deployment infrastructure Running jobs that require human interaction (for example, indexing 1 million web pages) Creating the required product or component configurations for different purposes Verification of system qualities (for example, performance and vulnerability testing) Enabler stories are demonstrated just like user stories, typically by showing the knowledge gained, artifacts produced, or the user interface, stub, or mock-up.
