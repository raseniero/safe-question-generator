Because they focus on the user as the subject of interest and not the system, user stories are value and customer-centric. To support this, the recommended form of expression is the ‘user-voice form,’ as follows: As a (user role), I want to (activity) so that (business value) By using this format, the teams are guided to understand who is using the system, what they are doing with it, and why they are doing it. Applying the ‘user voice’ format routinely tends to increase the team’s domain competence; they come to better understand the real business needs of their user.
