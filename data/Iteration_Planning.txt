Iteration planning is a SAFe Scrum event where all team members determine how much of the Team Backlog they can commit to delivering during an upcoming Iteration. The team summarizes this work as a set of committed iteration goals. Iteration planning is the first event of the Iteration. During planning, the team defines, organizes, and commits to the work for the next iteration. The iteration planning meeting is timeboxed to approximately 90 minutes for a two-week iteration. The team’s backlog has been partially identified and planned during PI Planning. In addition, the teams have feedback—not only from their prior iterations but also from the System Demo, stakeholders, and others. All this context feeds into the iteration planning event to inform the plan for the upcoming iteration.
